# Tempo

## What it is
A personal web app (installable to the iPhone home screen) that lines up the songs you played during a workout with your pace and heart rate, so you can see which songs make you faster.

## Who it's for
The owner first: a runner/cyclist/walker who records workouts on Apple Watch → Strava and listens on Spotify and Apple Music. Built to go public later.

## Surfaces and mode
App UI, **Operate** mode: workout list, workout detail (pace + heart-rate timeline with song bands), Power songs ranking, Heart-rate ranking, Settings/connect.

## Use scene
Right after a workout, on a phone, often outdoors and in daylight, still a bit out of breath. Glanceable, upbeat, rewarding.

## Product truths
- Sources: Spotify (recently played, last 50 only, must sync after each workout) and Strava (streams). Apple Music needs its data export or Last.fm (not built yet).
- Songs are compared against the workout's own average, warm-up (first 5 min) excluded.
- Analysis only: no playlist generation or live coaching.
- All data stays in the browser.

## Brand brief (from the owner)
Sky blue as the main colour, white text and buttons, hot pink for accents and details, a soft gradient landscape, animations. Music-first touches (album art) welcome.
